# CedarView privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

CedarView is an unofficial app, made by a student, that shows your own myCU
information (chapel skips, meal-plan balances, dining menus, chapel schedule)
on your Android phone. It is not made or endorsed by Cedarville University.

## The short version

CedarView has no servers of its own. It does not collect, sell, share or
analyse your data, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking. Everything it
shows is fetched straight from Cedarville's own websites to your phone, and
stays on your phone.

## What the app accesses

- **Your Cedarville sign-in.** You sign in on Microsoft's own sign-in page,
  shown inside the app. CedarView never sees, receives or stores your
  password. After you sign in, the sign-in session (a cookie) is kept by
  Android's built-in WebView in the app's private storage, just as a browser
  would keep it.
- **Your myCU records.** The app reads your chapel attendance and meal-plan
  balances from `selfservice.cedarville.edu` using that session, and shows
  them on screen.
- **Public Cedarville information.** Dining menus
  (`diningdata.cedarville.edu`) and the chapel schedule
  (`mediaserve.cedarville.edu`) are public and fetched without your account.

The app contacts only those Cedarville addresses and Microsoft's sign-in
service (`login.microsoftonline.com`). Nothing is sent to the developer or to
anyone else.

## What is stored on your phone

- The sign-in session cookie, held by Android's WebView.
- A small file with timestamps (such as when you last signed in) and which term
  you were last viewing, so the app opens where you left off. It contains no
  credentials.
- Your light/dark theme preference.

All of it lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
Android may include app data in your device backup if you have backups turned
on. **Sign out** in the app's menu signs you out through Microsoft and clears
the app's saved information. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in
Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Permissions

CedarView asks for one permission, **Internet access**, to reach the
Cedarville sites above. It does not access your location, contacts, camera,
files or anything else on your phone.

## Children

CedarView is meant for Cedarville University students and is not directed at
children.
